Before we formulate the algorithm, let's first derive the
expression for the 1st - 4th order derivatives for an arbitrary
point (a,b) in a coordinate system (x,y).

[figure labels: y, x, (0,0), (a,b), (x1,y1), (x2,y2), (x3,y3), (x4,y4), (x5,y5), (x6,y6)]

and (a,b) does not go through any (xi,yi), where (xi,yi)
is the location of pixel center and "i" represents the
"i'th" pixel surrounding (a,b).

The Taylor expansion at (a,b) can be written as

f(x,y) = f(a,b) + f_x(a,b)(x-a) + f_y(a,b)(y-b)
+ f_xx(a,b)/2 (x-a)^2 + f_xy(a,b)(x-a)(y-b) + f_yy(a,b)/2 (y-b)^2
+ f_xxx(a,b)/6 (x-a)^3 + f_yyy(a,b)/6 (y-b)^3 + f_xyy(a,b)/2 (x-a)(y-b)^2 + f_xxy(a,b)/2 (x-a)^2(y-b)
+ f_xxxx(a,b)/24 (x-a)^4 + f_yyyy(a,b)/24 (y-b)^4 + f_xxxy(a,b)/6 (x-a)^3(y-b) + f_xyyy(a,b)/6 (x-a)(y-b)^3 + f_xxyy(a,b)/4 (x-a)^2(y-b)^2   (1)

For shorthand notation, f(a,b) will be denoted by f^0
f_[unclear](a,b) will be denoted by f^0 x^3 y and likewise
